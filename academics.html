Our Academic Pathway

Academic Structure & Student Progression

A clear and structured journey from foundational learning to specialized excellence.

1

Junior Secondary (JSS 1–3)

Building strong foundations with a broad curriculum – Mathematics, English, Basic Science, Social Studies, CCA, PHE, and more – nurturing well‑rounded learners ready for the next stage.

2

Senior Secondary Year 1 (SS1)

Introduction to broader academic pathways – students explore Science, Arts, and Commercial subjects while strengthening core competencies.

3

Specialization (SS2 – SS3)

Students select their preferred field – Science, Arts, or Commercial – and dive deeper into specialized subjects, with focused WAEC/NECO preparation and career readiness.
